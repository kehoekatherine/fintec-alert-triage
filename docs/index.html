    What the numbers mean

    Baseline
    The rules engine in triage/engine.py. It does not call a model.
    Fitness
    Share of the escalate-everything cost the system avoids. A wrongful close costs 10. Escalating everything scores 0.
    Unsafe clearance
    Closing a case that should have been an RFI or an escalation.
    Overfitting
    Train score minus held-out score. The engine was not trained on the hand labels.
    Circular test
    The classical models were trained on the engine's own verdicts. A high score means they copied the policy. It is not an official unseen score.
