What it costs to triage with a model

Prices were read from provider pages on 26 Nov 2026. Token counts are estimated from the 4,000 development cases (characters ÷ 4). No model was called, so accuracy cells stay empty until you drop measurements into docs/data/llm_benchmarks.json.

Definitions

Baseline
The deterministic rules engine in triage/engine.py. It does not call a model. Every comparison below is against that system.
Fitness
The brief’s cost-matrix score out of 100: the share of the cost of escalating every case that the system avoids. A wrongful close costs 10, missing a close costs 3, an RFI on a true escalation costs 3. Escalating everything scores 0.
Accuracy
Share of cases with the right verdict, and share with the right reason code. On this page the denominator is a hand-labelled stratified sample, not the official answer key.
Unsafe clearance
Wrongful CLOSE rate: cases labelled RFI or ESCALATE that the system closed. That is the cost-10 error.
Overfitting
Absolute gap in fitness between the even and odd halves of the hand-labelled sample. The engine was not trained on those labels. A gap of 0 here is not an unseen-test score.
Cost
Model list price × estimated tokens. The rules engine’s marginal token cost is zero.
Workload
Analyst minutes avoided when a case is closed by the system. RFIs and escalations are assumed to stay with a person. Minutes and the hourly rate are sliders.
Customer value
Analyst pounds saved, closes that were not sent back as an RFI, and held payments the rules engine released. Held-payment value per payment is a slider because the dataset has no customer-loss figure.
